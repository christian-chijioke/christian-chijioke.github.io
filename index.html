About Me

Passionate about turning data into decisions that matter.

I am a researcher, data scientist, and innovator with a deep commitment to solving complex, real-world problems through the power of data and intelligent systems. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, data analytics, and applied research — with a particular focus on impact-driven solutions for the African context.

I bring rigorous analytical thinking to every challenge, whether that means building predictive models, uncovering insights from messy datasets, or translating research findings into actionable strategies for organisations and communities.

I am equally passionate about knowledge sharing, mentorship, and building a stronger data science ecosystem across Africa.

Research

Publications

Efficient Neural Architecture Search for Resource-Constrained Devices

We introduce ConstrainedNAS, a novel framework for discovering neural network architectures that achieve state-of-the-art accuracy while respecting strict computational budgets — enabling deployment on edge devices prevalent in emerging markets.

Read Paper →

FedSavanna: Federated Learning Under Intermittent Connectivity in Sub-Saharan Africa

This paper presents a federated learning protocol designed to tolerate the high packet-loss rates and intermittent connectivity characteristic of rural African networks, achieving robust model convergence without centralised data collection.

Read Paper →

AfroLM: A Self-Supervised Language Model for 25 African Languages

We release AfroLM, a multilingual transformer pre-trained on a curated corpus of 25 African languages. AfroLM achieves new benchmarks on downstream NLP tasks while requiring significantly less compute than existing multilingual models.

Read Paper →
Work

Selected Projects

NaijaHealth AI — Clinical Decision Support System

A diagnostic AI assistant deployed across 14 primary healthcare centres in Nigeria. The system assists clinicians in diagnosing malaria, tuberculosis, and diabetic retinopathy using low-cost smartphone cameras, serving over 40,000 patients to date.

View Project →

GridSense — Predictive Maintenance for Solar Micro-Grids

An IoT + ML platform that monitors off-grid solar installations across rural communities, predicting component failures up to 72 hours in advance. Deployed in partnership with the Rural Electrification Agency, reducing downtime by 61%.

View Project →

AfricaML Toolkit — Open-Source Low-Compute ML Library

A Python library providing efficient, well-documented implementations of classical and modern ML algorithms optimised for low-RAM environments. Actively maintained with 2,800+ GitHub stars and used by researchers in 30+ countries.
